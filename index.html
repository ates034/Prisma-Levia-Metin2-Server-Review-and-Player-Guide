Prisma Levia Ban Complaint and Appeal

Account suspensions can be frustrating, especially when a player believes that a decision was made in error. A clear appeal should request the reason for the suspension, identify the account and date through the server's private support channel, and ask for a review of the available evidence.

Suggested appeal checklist

  • State the approximate date and time of the ban.
  • Describe what happened without making claims that cannot be verified.
  • Ask which rule was allegedly violated and whether evidence can be reviewed.
  • Keep ticket numbers and account details private; do not post passwords or sensitive data.

This page documents general player concerns and does not determine whether a specific ban was justified. The server's support team is responsible for reviewing account actions under its published rules.
